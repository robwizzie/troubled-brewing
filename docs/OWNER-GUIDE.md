# Owner Guide — Editing Your Website

A plain-English, printable reference for Tom, Cat & Katie. It matches the in-app **Help** page. You never need to touch code to run your site.

## The one rule

**Nothing goes on the website until you press Publish.** Change whatever you like — only you can see it. When it looks right, press **Publish** (on the chalkboard screen the button says **Put it on the website**). Changed your mind? Press **Discard**. If you try to leave with changes waiting, it asks first.

## One-page how-to

Go to **troublebrewingcoffeehouse.com/admin** and sign in. You land on **Admin home**. Every screen has **← Admin home** at the top to get back, and on a phone **☰ Menu** (top left) lists every screen.

| I want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Put today's chalkboard on the website** | Admin home → **📷 Update today's chalkboard** → **Take a photo of the board** → check the preview → **Put it on the website**. Done. |
| **Change hours or add a holiday closure** | Admin home → **Change hours or add a holiday closure**. Change the times, or pick a date + name and press **+ Add holiday**. Press **Publish**. |
| **Post an announcement banner** | Admin home → **Post an announcement banner**. Type the message, tick **Show the banner**, press **Publish**. Untick it and Publish to take it down. |
| **Edit a menu description or photo** | Admin home → **Edit the menu descriptions or photos** → **Edit** on the item → change it → **Done** → **Publish**. (Names and prices come from SpotOn.) |
| **Add an event** | Admin home → **Add an event** → **+ Add event** → fill it in → **Done** → **Publish**. Past events drop off by themselves. |
| **Change words or photos on a page** | Admin home → **Edit a page's words or photos**. Pick the page at the top, tap the part you want to change, type in the panel. Press **Publish**. |
| **Hide or feature a review** | Admin home → **Hide or feature a review**. **🚫 Hide** or **☆ Feature** a review, or set **Minimum stars**. Press **Publish**. |
| **Can't find it?** | Type it in **What do you want to change?** on Admin home — "closed for snow", "latte photo", "open mic", "new barista" — and tap the match. |

**Undo:** before you publish, press **Discard**. After you publish, open the item (or tap the section) → **Earlier versions** → **Use this version** → **Publish**.

## Today's chalkboard, step by step

1. On Admin home, tap the big **📷 Update today's chalkboard** card (it's also second in **☰ Menu**). The top of the screen tells you what's on the website now — e.g. *"On the website now — taken this morning at 7:42, comes down Thursday"* or *"Not showing — the drawn menu board is up"*.
2. Tap **Take a photo of the board**. Your phone's camera opens. Snap the board. (Already took one? Use **or choose a photo you already took**.)
3. You'll see exactly how the homepage will look — the board in its wooden frame, tilted, with *"Updated today"*. Don't like it? **🔄 Retake**.
4. Optional: **Add a few words under the photo** ("Pumpkin cold foam is back!").
5. Tap **Put it on the website**. You'll see **It's on the website!** and a **See it on the website** button.

**How long should it stay up?** Pick under the photo. Usually **3 days**, then the homepage goes back to the drawn board of your live menu, so old specials never linger. Choose **Until I replace it** and the photo stays up until you post a new one (the top line then says *"Showing since Oct 19 · stays up until replaced"*). Under **More options**: *The board hasn't changed — keep this photo up longer*, and *Take the photo down*. All of these wait for the big button too.

**Past boards.** Every photo you put up is kept. Once there are two or more, the homepage shows **See past boards** under the chalkboard, a flip-through of the old boards (also at `/chalkboard`). Blurry one? Open **Past boards** at the bottom of the chalkboard screen, tap **Hide**, then **Update past boards on the website**. The photo stays saved; it just won't show. **Find older board photos** adds any earlier board photos still in storage.

**If something goes wrong** the screen says so in plain words: *photo too big* (take it with the camera button — those always fit), *no connection* (check the Wi-Fi and tap the button again — your photo is still there), *signed out* (tap **Sign in again**, then come back and tap the button). **iPhone photos:** taking the photo with the button always works. A HEIC photo copied to a laptop only opens in Safari — if you see the HEIC message, do it from the phone, or set iPhone **Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible**.

## Getting around

- **Admin home** — the chalkboard card, the search box, a card for each common job, anything **Waiting to publish**, and what was **Recently published** from this phone or computer. First time? A three-step tip explains the basics; tap **Got it — hide this** to put it away.
- **Sidebar / ☰ Menu** — Home, Today's chalkboard, Edit your pages, then *Every day* (Hours & holidays, Banner & links, Messages) and *Lists on the site* (Menu, Events, Google reviews). Everything else (quotes, Gallery Wall, Troublemakers, Local Love, Timeline, Photo library, Google connection) is under **More**.
- Buttons mean the same thing everywhere: **+ Add …** adds, **Edit** opens, **Done** keeps your change on the screen, **Delete** asks first and can be undone until you **Publish**.
- Anything technical (Google IDs, links to photos) is tucked under **Advanced** — you shouldn't need it.

> 💡 Everything works on a phone. The page editor stacks on a small screen: your page on top, the fields for whatever you tapped underneath, the Publish bar along the bottom.

# More detail

### Add or edit a menu item
**The menu keeps itself in sync with SpotOn.** Every morning the site reads your SpotOn ordering menu and updates itself — new items, price changes, things you 86'd, all of it. So for names, prices, and what's available, **just edit SpotOn like you already do** and the site follows.

What's still yours to polish here: **descriptions, photos, and dietary tags** — the fun wording and pictures never get overwritten. Admin home → **Edit the menu descriptions or photos**, or in the page editor go to the **Menu** page, tap the menu and press **Manage menu →**.

### Hours and holidays
The homepage welcome sign shows **Open now / Closed** with today's closing (or next opening) time automatically, straight from your hours — always on New Jersey time. If your Google Business hours are connected, they win; update regular hours on Google and use this screen for holiday closures.

### Add a Troublemaker (team member)
Go to the **Troublemakers** page, click the team grid, press **Manage Troublemakers →**. Photo, name, role, short bio, fun facts. Mark someone inactive when they move on (keeps their record).

### Add a piece to the Gallery Wall
Go to the **Gallery Wall** page, click the collection, press **Manage pieces →**.

Fill in **Who made it** and **Link for the artist** whenever you know them — the artist's name shows under the piece and links wherever you point it. That credit is the point of that page. You can also note what it is (“Oil on canvas”), roughly when it's from, which frame it hangs in, and whether it's for sale. Everything except the title is optional.

**Put a piece on the wall photo.** The top of that page is a photograph of your actual wall, and every frame in it can be clicked — a visitor picks one, the photo zooms into it, and a card comes up with whatever you've written. The last field on each piece, **Where it hangs on the wall photo**, is what connects the two: choose the frame in the photo that this piece really is (“The Rubber Ducks”, “The Gold Fox”, “Fox on a Bicycle”…) and your artist, credits, story and photo appear when someone opens it. Until you do, that frame just describes itself and says the story isn't written down yet — which is true, and better than making one up. Leave it blank for anything not in the photo.

### Add a local business to Local Love
Go to **Local Love**, click the grid, press **Manage Local Love →**.

Give each one a **street address** and the page arranges itself as a walk down Station Ave, in number order, with your own door marked in the right place — you never have to sort the list. Ask them for a **logo** (most places are glad to be featured; without one we draw their initials, which looks fine), and fill in **What you send people for** — the specific thing, like “the vodka rigatoni”. That's the line customers actually act on.

### Choose which Google reviews show
Admin home → **Hide or feature a review** (or click the reviews strip on your homepage and press **Manage reviews →**). Every review Google has sent us is listed there.

- **Minimum stars** is the big one: set it to *4★ and up* and nothing below that appears anywhere on the site. That's the whole "only show the good ones" switch.
- **🚫 Hide** takes one review off the site without changing the star minimum.
- **☆ Feature** puts a review first, everywhere it appears.
- **📷 Add photo** — Google doesn't let us fetch the pictures people attach to reviews, so save the photo from the review on Google and upload it here. Reviews with a photo lead the homepage strip and get their own **With photos** filter on the Reviews page.
- **+ Favorite** copies a review into your hand-picked wall on the Reviews page, where you can edit its wording.

New reviews arrive on their own — Google gives us five at a time, so the list fills out over the following days. Your live rating appears automatically.

### Write your own featured quote
Go to the **Reviews** page, click the testimonials wall, press **Manage testimonials →**. Paste the quote, the reviewer's first name, stars, and mark your favorites **Featured**.

### Rearrange, hide, or remove sections
With nothing selected, the right panel lists every section on the page in order: ▲▼ to reorder, 👁 to hide/show, 🗑 to remove, **+ Add a section** at the bottom. You see the change straight away; it goes live when you press **Publish** (Undo works until then).

### Swap the homepage look
On the **Home** page, click the big hero at the top. The panel shows **Homepage look** — Gallery Wall, Warm Storefront, Cozy Editorial, or Modern Coffee. Pick one and the page swaps on your screen; **Publish** makes it the look everyone sees. Each look has its own words and photos you can edit right below.

### Change the "Current Drink Specials" link on the homepage
With the **Gallery Wall** look active, click the hero → **Specials link text** / **Specials link URL**. Out of the box it opens your menu on the **Specialty** tab (`/menu#specials`). Point it anywhere — `/menu#seasonal`, or a full URL (e.g. an Instagram post).

### Pick a frame style for a homepage frame
Click the hero (Gallery Wall look) → each frame has a **Frame style** menu with vintage looks matched to your real wall. Like the shop wall, it looks best when no two frames share a style.

### Change a photo or label on the homepage wall (Immersive Gallery look)
Click the hero, then scroll the panel to **The frames on your wall**. Every frame is listed — swap its photo, rename its label, point it somewhere else, or change its molding. Leave a box blank to keep the original, or press **Reset**. These are the same frames phone visitors see hung as real framed pictures, so one change fixes both.

### Change the drinks shown on the homepage
Click the **Signature sips** row on the homepage. Either name the drinks you want (exactly as they're spelled on your menu), or leave that blank and choose a category to feature from and how many to show. They come live from your Menu, so the price, description and photo always match the Menu page — change it once, it changes in both places.

### Add a photo to a drink
**Manage menu →** the item → **Photo**. That single upload shows on the Menu page *and* in the homepage drinks row. Once any item has a photo, the Menu page switches itself from the classic price list to photo cards — the same cards the homepage uses. Drink personalities (the shareable cards after the fox's quiz) only appear for drinks that have a photo, so adding drink photos here unlocks more of them.

### Read messages from customers
Sidebar → **Messages** — contact and catering submissions land here. Filter by type, mark read/unread. (An unread count shows next to Messages.)

### The announcement banner
Admin home → **Post an announcement banner** — type a short message ("Closed early Sat for a private event"), tick **Show the banner** and press **Publish**. It shows at the top of every page until you untick it and Publish again.

## Photos: keep them snappy
Upload straight from your phone — the site automatically shrinks large photos so your pages stay fast. Landscape shots usually look best for heroes and cards.

## Need help?
The in-app **Help** page (sidebar → Help) has step-by-step guides. If you're ever stuck, reach out to your developer — and remember, **Earlier versions → Use this version → Publish** can undo almost anything.
